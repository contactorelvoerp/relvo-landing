# Inputs and Interview Questions

Each item is a piece of data the offer needs. First check whether it's in the code. Ask only about missing or uncertain items. If the user doesn't know — propose 2–3 options to choose from.

## Round 0 — Language (only if not determined in Step 1a)
0. **Copy language**: In which language should the website copy be written? (Offer detected options as choices, e.g. the site's locales.) For multi-locale sites: which is the primary language, and should other locales be written too or left for translation?
0a. **Form of address**: e.g. Polish "Ty"/"Państwo", German "du"/"Sie", French "tu"/"vous" — only if not inferable from existing copy.

## Round 1 — Customer and dream outcome
1. **Avatar**: Who exactly buys? (industry/role/life situation, company size, level of sophistication). Who is NOT a good customer?
2. **Dream outcome**: What does the customer want to achieve — in their own words? What does their life/business look like after success? (concrete: number, state, timeframe)
3. **Pain**: What hurts them most today? What does not solving it cost them (money, time, stress, status)?
4. **Previous attempts**: What have they already tried and why didn't it work?

## Round 2 — Product and delivery
5. **What we sell**: physical / digital product / service / SaaS / course / coaching / hybrid?
6. **Mechanism**: Why does it work? Is there a named method/process?
7. **Delivery**: How does the customer get the result (1:1, group, self-serve, done-for-you, software)? How long does it take?
8. **Time to first result**: When will the customer see the first result?
9. **Customer effort**: What must the customer do themselves? What can we do for them?

## Round 3 — Proof and obstacles
10. **Proof**: testimonials, case studies, customer counts, results, media, certifications, experience. (Real only — missing = placeholder.)
11. **Objections**: What do prospects say before they buy/decline? Why do people drop off?
12. **Post-purchase obstacles**: Where do customers get stuck during implementation?

## Round 4 — Constraints and risk
13. **Capacity**: How many customers can you realistically serve per month/cohort? Is there a unit limit?
14. **Deadlines**: Are there natural deadlines (cohort start, season, shipping, price change)?
15. **Guarantee**: Which guarantee will the business accept? (unconditional refund within X days / conditional on outcome / work until it works / anti-guarantee for non-refundable services). What refund rate is acceptable?
16. **Existing bonuses/assets**: What materials, templates, tools, or access do you already have (cheap to deliver, valuable to the customer)?

## Round 5 — Pricing
17. **Current price / range** and model (one-time, subscription, installments).
18. **Margin and delivery cost** per unit — to judge which bonuses and packages you can afford.
19. **Multi-unit buying**: Do customers buy repeatedly / multiple units / multiple sessions / for a team? (determines quantity packages)
20. **Market**: target country/market and currency.

## Minimum set to start
If the user wants to move fast, these are strictly required: copy language (0), 1, 2, 5, 7, 13 (or 14), 15, 17, 20. Fill in the rest with proposals marked as assumptions to verify.
